Waxing, brows & makeup in Kfarchima

L'ICÔNE is more than nails. Eyebrows are $5, full legs wax is $15 and full makeup is $50. Here is everything we do for your face and body.

Eyebrows and face

Eyebrows are $5 and moustache (upper lip) is $2. For the rest of the face, the facial hair trimmer is $10. Book them together, or add them to a nail appointment.

Waxing

We wax legs, arms and underarms. Pick only the areas you need:

  • Legs: full legs $15, half legs $10.
  • Arms: full arms $10, half arms $7.
  • Underarms: $5.

We no longer offer full body waxing.

Makeup

Full makeup is $50, for a wedding, an engagement, a night out or a photo shoot. Tell us the date, the time and the look you want when you book, and send a photo if you have one.

Lashes

Fake lashes are $10. Cluster lashes are $15: small groups of lashes placed along your own for a fuller, more natural look. Add either one to your makeup.
