Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, please book first. Send a WhatsApp message to +961 76 048 557 with the service you want and a day that suits you, and we'll reply with a time.

How much is a manicure at L'ICÔNE?

A classic manicure is $7. A manicure with gel color is $15, and regular polish on its own (pose vernis) is $5 to $6. Everything else is on the price list.

Gel or acrylic extensions: which should I choose?

Both add length. Gel extensions ($50) feel lighter and look glossier. Acrylic ($60) is harder, which suits long and stiletto shapes. If you only want your natural nails stronger, rubber base ($15) is usually enough.

How long does gel color last?

Usually two to three weeks, depending on how fast your nails grow and how hard you are on your hands. Rubber base under the color helps it hold on thin or soft nails.

Do you do nail art?

Yes. Hand-painted designs are $1 to $3 extra and chrome is $5 extra, on top of the base service. Send us a photo of the set you have in mind when you book.

Where is the salon?

In Kfarchima (also written Kfarshima), Baabda District, close to Hadath and Choueifat. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the location pin.
