Manicure

Shaping, cuticle care and a clean, even finish, for $7. Add gel color and it's $15 for a manicure that stays glossy for weeks.

Pose vernis

Add gel color to a pedicure for $20. On toes it can last a month or more.

False nails

Manicure & pedicure questions

How much is a manicure in Kfarchima?

At L'ICÔNE a manicure is $7, regular polish without a manicure (pose vernis) is $5 to $6, and a manicure with gel color is $15.

What's the difference between a dry pedicure and a spa pedicure?

A dry pedicure ($10) is done without soaking your feet in water. A spa pedicure ($15) includes a foot soak and more time on the heels and skin.

Can I get gel color on my toes?

Yes. A pedicure with gel color is $20. It lasts much longer on toes than regular polish.
